## Property Domain - Model and Search Strategy

### Status
Accepted

## Context
Property search req includes: City, ava dates, num of guests, and keywords.
Firestore has significant limitations compared to a relational database:

1. Dose Not support geo queries (no Range X km from point Y)
2. Dose not date range overlap as a single query on 2 fields
3. Dose not support OR on different fields in one query
4. Dose not support full text search on string fields.

This limitations impact directly on the design of PropertyRepo

## Decision
**Search by city**: Firestore query `whereEqualTo("city",city)`. Simple and native support. Accepted limit: Lookup by exact city not range

**Capacity Filter**: `whereGreaterThanOrEqualTo("capacity",capacity)`, compound `(city,capacity)`

**Keyword Filter**: executed client side after firestore query 

**Date Avalibility**: not filtered at firestore level per property. Avaliablity check happends on booking domain(overlapcheck).an property appears in search , even if it has active bookings, its responsability of property details to show non avalibable dates.

## Consequences

**Pros**
-   Functional Search without additional infra.
-  Minimal Firestore Indexes covers 90% use cases
**Tradeoffs**
- No Range search implmeneted. Would require an dedicated backend with the correct support.

**Technical Debt**
-   Keyword filter inmemory dose not scale well, it can work but for a relativly low scale. with 1k+ properties , per city , download all documents, filter client side its not acceptable latency wise and cost wise (firestore bills on rd). Could be mitigated via Elasticsearch or algolia for full text search.
